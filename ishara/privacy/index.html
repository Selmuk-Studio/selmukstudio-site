Ishara — Privacy Policy

Selmuk Studio · Last updated: 2026

Ishara is a party game made by Selmuk Studio. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, phone number or contacts.

Information kept on your phone

Your settings, team names, the custom words you type and your unlocked packs are saved only on your device. We don't receive them. If you use “Share our words”, the words are turned into a code that you choose where to send.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the UK and the European Economic Area) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → “Ad privacy choices”. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. Buying “All packs” removes ads.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play / App Store)

Word packs are bought through Google Play or the App Store. They process your payment; we never see your payment details. The app only checks which packs your store account owns.

Children

Ishara is a family party game but is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Purchases stay with your store account and can be restored.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
